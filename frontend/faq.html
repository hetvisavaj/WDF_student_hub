Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my portal password?

Navigate to the Login page and select "Forgot Password" to receive a password reset link at your registered email.

Where can I check assignment feedback?

Visit your Gradebook section via the Student Dashboard to view scores and rubric evaluations.

How can I register for an event?

Go to the Events page, select the event you want to attend, and click the Register button.

How do I update my student profile?

Open the Profile page and select Edit Profile to update your personal and academic information.
